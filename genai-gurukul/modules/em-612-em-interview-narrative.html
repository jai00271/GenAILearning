1. EM loop map — people / project / technical / behavioral

Typical large-tech EM loop (shapes vary): people management (coaching, conflict, performance), project/delivery execution, technical judgment (systems + GenAI trade-offs), behavioral / leadership principles. Kabhi bar-raiser / culture add-on. Prepare one flagship story per quadrant, plus one failure story with learning (EM 610/611).

2. STAR without fake numbers

Situation · Task · Action · Result. Result mein: qualitative outcomes OK (reduced manual toil via ServiceNow automation; clearer identity service ownership at scale themes); quantitative only if aap verify kar sakte ho. Agar CV pe ~95% on-time hai, interview mein bolo: “My operating claim is ~95% on-time for the period I tracked — here’s how I defined on-time and how I’d re-measure.” Invented “saved $2.4M” = credibility suicide at Director-caliber panels.

Pitfall

GenAI buzzword salad without eval/cost ownership story. Doosra: “I managed 5 people” ke baad silence on coaching conflict (EM 603/605). Teesra: copying First Round essays verbatim — paraphrase your life, cite guides.

3. Convert YOUR bullets — worksheet

  • ~5 engineers: span of control, how you split platform vs app work (609).
  • Velocity / on-time claim: definition, trade-offs vs quality/safety (610).
  • ServiceNow automation: toil removed, who benefited (Mutual Benefit).
  • Dell identity scale themes: reliability, stakeholder map (607), incidents (611).
  • Phase 1 GenAI: sequencing judgment — eval before RAG/FT storytelling.
Hands-on · STAR conversion table

5 rows: bullet → Situation → Task → Actions (3 bullets max) → Result (evidence type: metric claim to verify / qualitative / artifact). No new numbers allowed that aren’t already in your materials.

4. Director-level criteria (what “senior EM” sounds like)

Directors listen for: org design instincts, multi-team influence, hiring bar ownership, bad-news speed, technical taste without IC cosplay, and narrative coherence (“I optimize for Mutual Benefit / customer trust”). Scope beyond 5 people socho via EM Challenge (5→12) — interview mein future-state hypothetically, past tense lies mat bolo.

Portfolio-facing English · Narrative one-pager

Write ≤1 page: leadership purpose (Mutual Benefit in your words), three proof pillars grounded in TM experience, one failure with changed habit, and why EM now. Mark any quantitative claim as “to verify.” Do not invent metrics.

Director lens

Coherent story > polished jargon. Jai / Mritunjai path: identity + automation + delivery discipline + GenAI literacy as judgment, not as “I fine-tuned GPT.”

Self-check · formative only

1. EM loop ke char common buckets?

Reveal answer

People, project/delivery, technical judgment, behavioral/leadership.

2. Fake metrics kyun lethal hain?

Reveal answer

Director panels probe definitions; invented ROI destroys trust. Prefer verify-able claims + qualitative outcomes.

3. ~95% on-time kaise bolo?

Reveal answer

As your stated operating claim: define on-time, period, caveats; offer how you’d re-measure — don’t present unaudited vanity as gospel.

4. One-pager ke char blocks?

Reveal answer

Purpose / Mutual Benefit; three proof pillars; failure→habit; why this level now.

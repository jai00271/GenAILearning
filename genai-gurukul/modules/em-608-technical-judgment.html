EM 608 · Technical Judgment for Engineering Managers

Best coder hone ki zaroorat nahi — blind spots miss mat karo

Recall · Phase 1 · EM 606–607

Phase 1 ne eval, RAG, agents, cost, guardrails sikhaye. EM 606–607 ne bets aur stakeholders. EM 608 unhein technical decision quality se wire karta hai: kaunsi bet architecturally sound hai, kaunsa debt intentional hai, kaunsa GenAI path eval ke bina hype hai.

1. Dive deep vs delegate — decision tree

Dive deep jab:

  • Blast radius high ho (security, data loss, multi-tenant isolation, money).
  • Team novel domain mein ho aur false confidence dikhe.
  • Cross-team contract shape ho raha ho — EM often translator hota hai.
  • You smell irreversible lock-in (vendor, schema, public API).

Delegate (with check-ins) jab:

  • Local refactor, well-understood service, strong owner present.
  • Learning opportunity intentionally given to rising engineer.
  • Your dive would steal oxygen from staffing/stakeholder work that only you can do.

2. Architecture review — facilitate, don’t peacock

EM as facilitator checklist:

  1. Problem / non-goals pehle — solution slides baad mein.
  2. Force alternatives (at least one seriously considered).
  3. Ask: failure modes, operability, cost, security, migration.
  4. Time-box debate; park ratholes to offline with owner.
  5. End with decision, ADRs/RFC link, open questions.

Peacock mode: aap sabse smart line bolte ho, room shut down. Facilitate mode: quiet senior speaks, junior’s risk heard, you summarize tradeoffs in plain language for Product/Security guests.

Playbook · 45-min arch review agenda

0–5 context · 5–15 proposal · 15–25 alternatives & risks · 25–35 deep dive on top risk · 35–40 decision / conditions · 40–45 owners + follow-ups. Pre-read mandatory; no-pre-read → reschedule (protects culture).

3. Tech debt budgeting — intentional, not shame

Debt zero fantasy hai. Budgeted debt healthy hai. Practical model:

  • Reserve a fixed % of investable weeks (e.g. 15–20%) for debt/risk paydown — EM 606 capacity math se cut.
  • Classify: safety debt (fix now) vs drag debt (schedule) vs leftover experiments (delete).
  • Each debt item: user/ops impact, cost to fix, cost of delay.
  • Never only “cleanup sprint” as guilt trip after feature binge — continuous line item.

4. RFC culture — writing scales judgment

RFCs (or ADRs) team ko EM-brain distribute karte hain. Lightweight template: 

# RFC: [title]
Status: draft | review | accepted | superseded
Context
Problem
Options (incl. do-nothing)
Proposal
Risks & mitigations
Rollout / rollback
Open questions
Decision

Culture rules: comments on ideas not people; silence ≠ consent — ask explicit LGTM from named reviewers; time-box review window; EM models brevity.

5. Staying credible without being the best coder

  • Read artifacts weekly: one design, one incident note, one customer/partner complaint.
  • Ask better questions than write more code: “what’s the rollback?”, “who pages?”
  • Keep a thin hands-on slice (bug, spike, threat model) so instincts don’t rot — but time-box it.
  • Credit experts publicly; never steal design authorship in steering.
  • Update your learning: Phase 1 GenAI vocabulary is now table stakes for EM interviews at large orgs.
Pitfall

“I used to be Staff, so my taste is law.” Taste without current context = fossil judgment. Doosra pitfall: full retreat from tech — become pure people/process manager; engineers stop inviting you to real decisions, then surprise outages land on your desk.

6. GenAI stack judgment — Phase 1 bridge

Jab team “let’s add an agent” bole, EM questions (production bar):

Claim EM judgment check
RAG over our docs Golden set? Recall@k? Tenant isolation? Citation/refusal behavior?
Fine-tune Why not prompt/RAG first? Data rights? Eval regression plan?
Multi-agent Single-agent failed how? Tool allowlist? Cost per trajectory?
New model vendor Security review? Data retention? Failover? Unit economics?
“Demo worked” Offline eval + failure modes (injection, cost blowup) documented?

Aapka identity/security muscle yahan superpower hai: treat LLM features like new trust boundaries — prompts, retrieved docs, tool outputs sab untrusted input ho sakte hain (PROD 403 / OWASP LLM themes). EM 609 team design mein yahi bar hiring aur rituals tak extend hoga.

Director lens

GenAI proposals pe main buzzwords nahi, eval + risk + cost sun’na chahta hoon. Jo EM Phase 1 language bol sakta hai bina pretend kiye ki woh research scientist hai — woh Apple/Walmart-class interviews mein bhi survive karta hai. Jo EM har AI idea ko fear se block karta hai, team shadow-IT pe chali jaati hai.

Portfolio-facing English · Decision stub

I dive when blast radius or irreversibility is high, facilitate architecture reviews around alternatives and failure modes, budget tech debt explicitly, scale judgment through RFCs, and gate GenAI work on eval, security, and cost — without needing to be the strongest coder in the room.

Self-check · formative only

1. Kab EM ko dive deep karna chahiye?

Reveal answer

High blast radius, high irreversibility, novel false confidence, ya cross-team contracts — attention budget ke hisaab se.

2. Architecture review facilitate karte waqt pehla focus?

Reveal answer

Problem/non-goals + alternatives + failure modes — peacock solutions nahi.

3. Tech debt budget ka healthy pattern?

Reveal answer

Fixed % investable weeks; classify safety vs drag; continuous line item, guilt cleanup sprint only nahi.

4. GenAI proposal pe EM ke minimum gates?

Reveal answer

Eval evidence, security/trust boundary, cost/failover — demo alone insufficient.

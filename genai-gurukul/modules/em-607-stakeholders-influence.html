EM 607 · Stakeholders, Influence & Org Reality

Politics cynicism nahi — maps, RACI, aur decision logs

Track: EM · Phase 2 Audience: Technical Manager → EM Prereq: EM 606 Roadmaps · Phase 1 GenAI Next: EM 608 Technical Judgment

Large-org EM job ka bada hissa code review se bahar hai: Product deadlines, Design tradeoffs, Security gates, Finance questions, peer EMs competing for the same platform slot. Is chapter ka thesis simple hai — influence = clarity + reliability + relationships, manipulative theater nahi.

Aap MBM-style principles / delivery culture se aate ho toh “politics” word pe allergy normal hai. Reframe: org reality = multiple legitimate incentives. EM ka kaam unhein surface karna, align karna, aur jab align na ho toh decision owner ko honest options dena.

Recall · EM 606 · EM 604

Roadmaps ne tradeoff language di. Hiring bar ne “who we let in” set kiya. Stakeholders chapter woh language org ke across chalata hai — especially jab aapke bets doosri teams ki calendars pe depend karti hain.

Stance guess mat chhupao — “unknown” likho aur 1:1 book karo. Influence ≠ title hamesha; kabhi kabhi staff engineer ya ops lead silent veto rakhta hai.

2. RACI — ambiguity ka antidote

RACI (Responsible / Accountable / Consulted / Informed) tab fail hota hai jab Accountable do log hoon, ya Consulted ko veto samajh liya jaye. Rules of thumb:

  • Exactly one A per decision type.
  • R can be multiple; still need a lead R.
  • C = input rights, not pocket veto — unless policy says otherwise (Security sign-off).
  • I = no surprise; async okay.
Decision A R C I
Quarter bet mix EM (+ Director confirm) EM + Tech Lead Product, peer EMs Team
Ship to prod (auth change) EM or owning TL per policy Engineers Security, SRE Support / adjacent teams
Vendor LLM for internal tool Director / designated AI owner EM + engineer Security, Legal, Privacy Team, Finance
Director lens

Jab do EMs “shared accountability” claim karte hain aur slip hota hai, Director dono ko weak maanta hai. Mujhe clear A chahiye — even if matrixed. Agar aap A ho, bad news pehle mujhe do; agar aap C ho, veto drama mat banao — principled objection + alternative do.

3. Upward management — boss ko usable banao

Aapka manager aapka megaphone + air-cover hai. Unhe train karo:

  • Updates: outcome → risk → ask (EM 606 pulse). Novels nahi.
  • Pre-wire big rooms: meeting se pehle 5-line brief.
  • Tell them how you like feedback and when you need cover.
  • Never let them be blindsided by your stakeholder.
## Pre-wire note (example)
Decision sought Friday: approve Bet B thin-slice vs slip Bet A.
Recommendation: thin-slice Bet B; protect auth Sev2 KR.
Risk if we do nothing: Security review blocks launch window.
Ask from you: endorse tradeoff in steering; I’ll handle Product 1:1.

4. Partner guilds — Product, Design, Security

Product: outcome owner; aap feasibility + sequencing owner. Conflict often date vs quality. Resolve with options, not veto-by-silence.

Design: user clarity; engineering constraints early share karo (latency, accessibility, empty states for AI failure).

Security / Identity: aapke background ka home field. Treat them as design partners for threat model, not gatekeepers to “get past.” Early threat sketch > late exception request.

5. Conflict scripts — heat kam, signal zyada

Conflict script skeleton: 

1) Intent: “I want us to hit [shared outcome].”
2) Observation: “Here’s what I’m seeing [fact, not motive].”
3) Impact: “If we continue, [risk/cost].”
4) Options: A / B / C with tradeoffs.
5) Ask: “Which do you want to sponsor — or what’s missing?”

Avoid: sarcasm on Slack, cc storms, “align offline” without calendar. Prefer: short live call + decision log entry.

Pitfall

Cynicism (“sab politics hai”) aapko sharp nahi, predictable banata hai — log aapse real info share karna band kar dete hain. Doosra extreme: naivety — assume everyone has same incentives. Map incentives explicitly; stay warm, stay clear.

6. Politics without cynicism — practical ethics

  • Credit publicly, critique privately (behavior, not identity).
  • Don’t trade in reputational gossip as currency.
  • Escalation = last tool after options shown, not first flex.
  • Document decisions so absentees aren’t “outmaneuvered.”
  • If you lost a call fairly, execute professionally — appeal with new data later.

GenAI features pe decision logs especially valuable: model vendor, data retention, human-in-loop — baad mein audit / incident pe yahi timeline bachati hai.

Playbook · 20-min conflict reset

Invite only people with A/R/C. Share 5-line brief 24h prior. Meeting: 5 min facts, 10 min options, 5 min decide + owner + log. If no decide: explicitly park with date — “meeting ended in vibes” banned.

Director lens

Main un EMs ko promote karta hoon jo cross-org trust build karte hain — Product unki dates pe believe karti hai, Security unki designs pe early engage karti hai. Influence demos se nahi, kept promises + clean escalations se banta hai.

Portfolio-facing English · Decision stub

I map stakeholders by influence and interest, keep a single Accountable on each decision type, pre-wire my manager with asks, partner with Product/Design/ Security in their dialect, and write decision logs so conflict becomes memory instead of mythology.

Self-check · formative only

1. RACI mein Accountable ke kitne log ideal hain?

Reveal answer

Exactly one A per decision type — shared A usually means no A.

2. Upward update ka short spine?

Reveal answer

Outcome → risk → ask; pre-wire before big rooms.

3. Conflict script ke paanch steps?

Reveal answer

Intent, observation, impact, options, ask.

4. Decision log kyun?

Reveal answer

Org memory, audit/incident readiness, aur “outmaneuvered absentee” pattern kam karne ke liye.

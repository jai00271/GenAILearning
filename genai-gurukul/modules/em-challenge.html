1. Challenge kya hai — aur kya nahi

Deliver a written leadership packet (English OK for portfolio sections; Hinglish planning notes allowed in your draft). Required pieces:

  1. Org redesign (5→12): platform enablement vs app squad(s), reporting lines, named ownership for eval harness, model/prompt gateway, cost meters, and at least one domain app surface.
  2. Hiring plan: roles, sequencing (fundamentals vs GenAI glitter), what you hire vs upskill, pairing backend-strong leads with ML specialists.
  3. 90-day plan: days 0–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 outcomes (staffing, standards, first eval beat, incident/Sev card, culture norms).
  4. Risk register: ≥6 risks (vendor lock-in, cost runaway, safety climate, on-call burnout, identity-adjacent harm, delivery pressure) with owner + mitigation.
  5. Eval & cost ownership: RACI-style clarity — who can ship prompt/model changes, who pays the bill, who stops the line.

Yeh CAP coding capstone nahi. Yeh multi-agent demo nahi. Agar aapne architecture diagram paste kiya bina owners ke, fail. Agar metrics invent kiye, fail.

2. Kaise approach karo

Pehle constraints freeze: budget honesty (unknowns OK), hiring lead times, what stays with you as EM vs what delegates. Phir ownership map (609). Culture norms one-pager (610). Sev/on-call sketch (611). Narrative paragraph linking to your real TM proof points (612) — still no fabricated numbers. MBM Mutual Benefit: show how customers, engineers, and company each gain from the redesign.

Hands-on · packet checklist

Create one folder or doc: org.md, hiring.md, 90day.md, risks.md, ownership.md, plus self-score table. Optional lab path under genai-gurukul-labs/challenges/em/ if present — otherwise this page is the brief.

Pitfall

“Hire 7 ML engineers” without sequencing or eval ownership. Or paste a fancy org chart with no risk register. Or claim industry-leading latency/cost wins you never measured. Challenge leadership judgment test karta hai.

3. Portfolio-facing English — deliverables

Portfolio-facing English · Leadership memo

Write a 1–2 page English memo summarizing the 5→12 redesign: org shape, hiring sequence, 90-day outcomes, top risks, and eval/cost ownership. State which quantitative claims are yours to verify (for example an on-time delivery operating claim) and which outcomes are qualitative (toil reduction themes, identity-scale reliability themes). End with reverse conditions: when you would delay hiring, keep a thinner platform, or refuse a vendor path.

Portfolio-facing English · Pass rule

Each dimension is scored from 1 to 4. You pass the EM track challenge only when no dimension is below 2 and the average across the four dimensions is at least 3.0. Anything less means revise the packet.

4. Rubric — score yourself

Dimension 1 — Inadequate 2 — Developing 3 — Solid 4 — Exemplary
Org & ownership design No clear platform/app split; eval/cost owners missing; chart without accountability. Split mentioned but ownership fuzzy; 5→12 growth not sequenced. Explicit platform vs app shape, named eval/cost/prompt-change owners, believable 5→12 path. Design would survive a director review: interfaces, escalation, and revisit triggers included.
People system (hire / culture / load) Headcount fantasy; ignores fundamentals vs GenAI glitter; no safety/on-call health. Hiring list exists but weak sequencing or culture/on-call afterthought. Hiring plan + upskill path + norms/on-call health addressed; Mutual Benefit visible. People plan matches delivery risk; pairing ML specialists with backend-strong leads is concrete.
Operating plan & risk No 90-day plan or empty risk list; ignores incidents/SLO reality. 90-day and risks present but generic or unowned. Phased 90-day outcomes and ≥6 owned risks with mitigations tied to GenAI ops. Plan reads executable; risks include identity-adjacent harm and cost runaway with stops.
Narrative integrity Invented metrics or buzzword-only story; ignores TM experience constraints. Some real themes, but claims overreach or skip verify framing. Grounded in TM themes; quantitative claims marked to verify; clear English memo. Coherent leadership narrative a Walmart/Apple-class panel could probe without collapse.

Definition of Done — EM challenge

  • Org redesign 5→12 with platform + app ownership named
  • Hiring plan + 90-day plan + risk register (≥6) + eval/cost RACI
  • English leadership memo; no fabricated metrics
  • Self-score on all four rubric dimensions; pass rule met (no dim < 2, avg ≥ 3.0)
  • Gate banner honored — leadership packet, not a coding solution folder

EM 606 · Roadmaps, OKRs & Delivery Ownership

Roadmap feature laundry nahi — bets + capacity + honest no

Track: EM · Phase 2 Audience: Technical Manager → EM Prereq: EM 605 Performance · Phase 1 GenAI Next: EM 607 Stakeholders & Influence

Delivery ownership ka matlab “jo bhi Jira pe likha hai, green bana do” nahi. Matlab: kya jeetna hai, kitna bandwidth hai, aur kis cheez ko consciously defer kar rahe ho — stakeholders ko dikhate hue. ~5-engineer team pe yeh aur sharp hota hai: har “chhoti request” ek sprint kha jaati hai.

Aapke paas microservices / identity delivery muscle already hai. EM bar pe shift yeh hai: aap personally hero ship nahi karte — aap system design karte ho jisme team predictably ship kare, dependencies visible rahein, aur GenAI experiments production tax ke saath balance hon.

Recall · EM 605 · PROD 404–406

EM 605 ne outcomes language diya. PROD track ne cost, deploy, CI/CD reality dikhai. EM 606 unhein roadmap math se jodta hai: OKRs bina capacity ke Wish.com ban jaate hain; WIP bina limit ke “busy” dikhta hai, done nahi.

1. Roadmap as bets, not a feature laundry list

Laundry list: 40 items, sab “P1”, koi kill criteria nahi. Bet: hypothesis + customer/ business outcome + time box + kill/scale rule. Ek quarter mein ek small team ke liye usually 2–4 real bets + keep-the-lights-on bucket. 

## Bet card — [Name]
Hypothesis: If we ___, then ___ will improve as measured by ___.
Customer / risk: ...
Time box: [e.g. 6 weeks]
Capacity reserved: [eng-weeks]
Dependencies: [teams / vendors]
Kill criteria: If by week N we don't see ___, we stop or pivot to ___.
Scale criteria: If ___, we invest next quarter in ___.
Not doing because of this bet: [...]
Director lens

Directors roadmap pe “coverage” nahi maangte pehle — coherence maangte hain. Teen unrelated hero features se better: ek theme (e.g. identity resilience) ke under sequenced bets. Unka follow-up hamesha: “agar yeh fail hua toh kya?” Kill criteria wala EM unke dimaag mein promote-ready lagta hai.

2. OKR pitfalls — theater vs steering

OKRs tab useful hain jab Objective direction de, Key Results outcomes measure karein. Theater mode:

  • KR = “ship feature X” (output disguised as KR).
  • 10+ KRs per team — none remembered mid-quarter.
  • 100% KR completion celebrated as excellence (sandbagging signal).
  • Shared KR without named owner → nobody owns.
  • GenAI KR = “explore LLMs” with no eval metric (Phase 1 ne yeh already reject kiya).

Steering mode example (illustrative): Objective — reduce auth-related Sev2 noise. KR — Sev2 auth incidents / month ↓ 40%; mean time to mitigate ↓ 25%; zero new undocumented service principals in owned fleet. Feature list KR ke neeche aati hai, jagah swap nahi.

3. Capacity math — pehle arithmetic, phir ambition

Rough eng-week model (5 engineers, 1 quarter ~12 weeks): 

Gross = 5 × 12 = 60 eng-weeks
− PTO / holidays (historical %) 
− Meetings / hiring / interrupts (be honest: often 15–25%)
− KTLO / on-call / debt minimum (protect this)
= Investable weeks for bets

Per bet: estimate range (p50 / p90), not a single fake number.
WIP load: if 3 bets × 3 people parallel > investable, you are lying on slides.

Identity / platform teams pe interrupt load under-count karna classic failure hai. Agar aapke pager pe weekly tax hai, roadmap se pehle usko line item banao — shame mat feel karo; hide mat karo.

4. WIP limits — busy ≠ delivery

Work-in-progress limit force karta hai finish > start. Team of 5 ke liye practical start: max 2 active bet streams + 1 interrupt swimlane. Review board weekly: blocked > 3 days? escalate or shed. Cycle time dashboard (even spreadsheet) ego slides se zyada honest hota hai.

Pitfall

“Sab parallel chalao, smart log hain.” Context switch tax invisible lagta hai jab tak incident season nahi aata. Doosra pitfall: roadmap freeze zero — change control ke bina har stakeholder veto; freeze absolute — market/risk ignore. Healthy: change budget (e.g. one bet swap per month with explicit trade).

5. Saying no — with alternatives

Pure no rare hota hai large orgs mein. EM-grade no:

  1. Acknowledge the outcome they want.
  2. Show capacity — what’s already funded.
  3. Offer alternatives — later date, thinner slice, different owner, kill another bet.
  4. Confirm decision owner — if they escalate, you already framed tradeoffs.
Script seed:
We want [outcome] too. This quarter investable weeks are on [Bet A/B] + KTLO.
Options: (1) thin slice by [date], (2) swap out [Bet B] — impact [X],
(3) park until next OKR cycle. Which tradeoff do you want to sponsor?
Playbook · Monday delivery review (30 min)

Board: Bet health (green/yellow/red) · blockers · dependency ETA · interrupt load last week · decision log (what we said no to). No status novels. Red without ask = incomplete. End with: “what we will stop or slip this week.” Capture in shared note Directors can skim in 90 seconds.

6. Dependency management

Dependencies fail quietly: “platform team will provide” without date, contract, or fallback. Ritual:

  • Name the human owner on the other side, not only the team name.
  • Interface contract: API/schema/SLA/date — written.
  • Fallback: mock, feature flag off, manual path — decide early.
  • Escalation clock: if slip > N days, who gets pinged with what ask.

Cross-team GenAI work pe specially: model/provider access, data classification, eval owners — yeh “engineering only” dependencies nahi; security/legal lanes early map karo (EM 607 deepen karega).

7. Delivery reviews — narrative for upward

Upward update template: 

## Delivery pulse — [Team] — [Week of]
Outcomes moved: ...
Risks (with owners): ...
Asks (one sentence each): ...
Tradeoffs taken: we deferred ___ to protect ___
Next demo / checkpoint: [date]

Directors hate surprise slips with no early yellow. Yellow early + ask = trust. Green until Friday red = career-limiting pattern.

Director lens

Main aapse “sab on track” nahi sun’na chahta — main aapse predictability + judgment sun’na chahta hoon. Jo EM bet kill karta hai jab data aata hai, woh mature hai. Jo EM har slip ko “dependency” pe daal deta hai bina apni WIP/scope control dikhaye, woh unreliable hai.

Portfolio-facing English · Decision stub

I run roadmaps as time-boxed bets with kill criteria, size OKRs to investable capacity after KTLO, limit WIP, refuse work only with explicit tradeoffs, and surface dependency contracts before the slip becomes a surprise.

Self-check · formative only

1. Bet card mein kill criteria kyun zaroori?

Reveal answer

Bina kill rule ke failing work zombie ban jaati hai; learning stop, capacity lock.

2. Investable weeks kaise nikalte ho?

Reveal answer

Gross eng-weeks − PTO − meeting/interrupt load − KTLO/on-call floor.

3. EM-grade “no” mein kya hona chahiye?

Reveal answer

Acknowledge outcome, show capacity, offer alternatives/trades, name decision owner.

4. Dependency hygiene ka minimum?

Reveal answer

Named human owner, written interface/date, fallback, escalation clock.

EM 605 · Performance, Levels & Comp Conversations

Rating sheet pe number nahi — expectations pe agreement

Track: EM · Phase 2 Audience: Technical Manager → EM Prereq: EM 604 Hiring Bar · Phase 1 GenAI Next: EM 606 Roadmaps & Delivery

Agar aap ~5 engineers lead karte ho — Koch-style delivery + mentoring mix — toh performance season aapka “soft skill” nahi, aapka operating system hai. Promo packet, PIP, calibration, aur comp conversation — yeh chaar rooms alag dialects bolte hain. Same person, same quarter, galat dialect = trust break.

Yeh chapter leveling ko conceptual rakhta hai (kisi company ke proprietary ladder ko copy nahi karta). Focus: expectations docs, coaching vs PIP, calibration hygiene, promo evidence, aur compensation talks jo dignity preserve karti hain. Aapka .NET / microservices / identity background yahan “tech depth signal” banega — lekin EM bar pe depth tabhi count hoti hai jab impact + scope clear ho.

Recall · EM 604 · CAP 502

Hiring bar ne “what good looks like at entry” set kiya. CAP 502 ne evidence-first narrative sikhaya. EM 605 wohi muscle andar ki taraf ghumata hai: team ke existing logon ke liye bar, trajectory, aur pay conversation — bina surprise ke.

1. Levels conceptually — ladder nahi, three questions

Har badi org apna ladder rakhti hai (IC3/IC4, L5/L6, Senior/Staff — names vary). Aapko un names ko rote karne ki zaroorat nahi. Director aur HR calibration mein usually teen sawaal chalte hain:

  1. Scope — problem kitni badi? team / multi-team / org?
  2. Ambiguity — requirements clear the ya aapne shape kiya?
  3. Leverage — impact sirf aapke code se, ya doosron ke through?

Same title alag companies mein alag mean karta hai. Isliye cross-company title matching dangerous hai. Apni org ke written level guide ko source of truth maano; bahar ke blogs ko intuition ke liye padho, packet language ke liye nahi.

Director lens

Director calibration mein “yeh banda smart hai” soft signal hai. Hard signal: kya yeh next-level problems already solve kar raha hai, without title? Agar aap wait kar rahe ho title ke baad responsibility dene ka — aap already late ho. Title usually peeche aata hai, peeche nahi.

2. Expectations doc — surprise killer

Performance conversation tab toxic hoti hai jab Q4 mein pehli baar bar dikhti hai. Expectations doc ek living one-pager hai — role + level ke hisaab se — jo 1:1s mein update hota hai. Template (copy, customize): 

## Expectations — [Name] · [Role/Level] · [Quarter]
Outcomes (3–5 measurable or observable):
- ...
Behaviors (how we work: reviews, on-call, partnering):
- ...
Growth bets (1–2 skills this quarter):
- ...
Not in scope / parked:
- ...
Evidence we will look at in review:
- PRs / designs / incident notes / partner feedback
Last updated: [date] · Agreed in 1:1: [yes/no]

GenAI Phase 1 complete karne wale TM ke liye tip: “learn RAG” growth bet nahi — “ship one evaluated retrieval path for X service with Recall@k documented” outcome hai. Vague learning goals calibration mein melt ho jaate hain.

3. Coaching vs PIP — alag instruments

Signal Coaching PIP / formal plan
Gap nature Skill / context / priority miss Sustained gap after clear expectations
Surprise? Should be low — 1:1 trail exists Must be zero — written trail required
Tone Development + accountability Clear bar, timeline, consequences
HR involvement Optional early Required per policy
Exit criterion Visible improvement trajectory Binary: meet written goals or exit path

Sabse common EM mistake: PIP ko “harsh coaching” samajhna. PIP legal/HR process hai — kindness ke saath, lekin ambiguity ke bina. Coaching loop short-circuit karke seedha PIP = culture damage + process risk. Ulta: clear underperformance pe months tak soft language = team pe tax, aur person ko unfair surprise.

Pitfall

Slack pe “need to step up” likh dena documentation nahi hai. Documentation = date, expectation stated, example observed, support offered, next check-in. Weaponizing notes (har mistake ka dossier bina coaching ke) trust kill karta hai. Under-documenting (sirf memory) aapko aur employee dono ko unprotected chhodta hai.

4. Calibration meetings — room ka physics

Calibration mein aapka job advocate-or-prosecutor nahi — evidence curator hai. Pehle se packet: outcomes, scope, peer/partner quotes (with permission), counter-examples aapne already coached. Room norms jo EM control kar sakta hai:

  • Compare against level bar, against “who I like” nahi.
  • Recency bias check: last two weeks ≠ whole half.
  • Visibility bias check: quiet backend owner vs flashy demo owner.
  • Identity / affinity bias: similar background ≠ higher rating.
Director lens

Jab aap har report ko “exceeds” laate ho, aapki currency inflate ho jaati hai. Jab aap kabhi kisi ko defend nahi karte, aap risk-averse dikhte ho. Healthy EM: mostly fair distributions + 1–2 fights worth fighting with evidence. Directors remember who wasted the room vs who clarified the bar.

5. Promo packets — evidence architecture

Promo packet ek sales deck nahi. Yeh case file hai. Recommended spine:

  1. Role / target level — org language exactly.
  2. Problem landscape — ambiguity + business stakes.
  3. What they owned — decisions, not only tickets closed.
  4. Outcomes — reliability, latency, cost, adoption, risk reduced.
  5. Leverage — mentorship, standards, unblocked teams.
  6. Growth edge — honest next bets (credibility booster).

Microservices / identity veterans ke liye trap: “I designed the auth flow” bina “what broke, what we measured, who else could run it now”. Staff-shaped work = system others can operate without you as single point of failure.

Playbook · 45-min promo draft

Engineer ke saath shared doc: 5 bullets outcomes, 3 bullets leverage, 1 risk they owned down. Aap add: scope framing + calibration vocabulary. Next 1:1: cut fluff, add dates/metrics. Manager-only section: your recommendation + residual concerns. Kabhi bhi engineer ko surprise “I don’t support yet” mat do promo week mein — woh conversation months pehle honi chahiye thi.

6. Comp conversations — hygiene over theater

Comp talk mein aap often messenger ho, author nahi — bands, budgets, HR guidelines. Hygiene checklist:

  • Separate performance narrative from budget constraints — conflate mat karo.
  • “Market” claims sirf jab aapke paas company-approved data ho; gossip bands mat quote karo.
  • Agar number disappoint kare: listen → restate facts → path (promo timing, scope, skills) → follow-up date.
  • Kabhi bhi “agar tum jaaoge toh…” matching promises mat do bina authority ke.
  • Private setting; no hallway comp comparisons.

Script seed (adapt, don’t recite): 

Thanks for the work this cycle — here’s how I rated outcomes vs expectations.
Compensation decision this round: [X]. Constraints I can share: [budget/band].
What I control with you next: [scope, visibility, promo readiness criteria].
What I will take upward: [specific ask]. Check-in date: [date].

7. Documenting without weaponizing

Notes ka ethical bar: future-you aur future-them dono ko fair trial do. Practical rules:

  • Write observable behavior + impact, not character labels (“lazy”, “attitude”).
  • Include support offered and employee response when relevant.
  • Share themes in 1:1; secret shadow file of petty gripes = mismanagement.
  • Positive evidence bhi likho — calibration aur memory both biased toward drama.
Portfolio-facing English · Decision stub

I keep a living expectations doc, coach before I formalize, bring artifacts not anecdotes into calibration, build promo cases on scope–ambiguity–leverage, and run comp talks that separate performance truth from budget constraints.

Self-check · formative only

1. Leveling ke teen conceptual axes kya hain?

Reveal answer

Scope, ambiguity, leverage — titles company-specific hain; axes portable hain.

2. Coaching aur PIP mein pehla structural farq?

Reveal answer

Coaching development + accountability; PIP formal written bar + timeline + consequences with HR process — surprise zero hona chahiye.

3. Calibration mein EM ka primary role?

Reveal answer

Evidence curator — bar ke against artifacts, storytelling contest nahi.

4. Comp talk hygiene ka ek non-negotiable?

Reveal answer

Performance narrative ko budget constraints se alag rakho; unauthorized matching promises mat do.

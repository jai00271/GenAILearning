EM 609 · Building GenAI Engineering Teams

GenAI team staff karna = “ML hire kar do” nahi — ownership map pehle

Track: EM · Phase 2 Audience: Technical Manager → Engineering Manager Prereq: EM 608 Technical Judgment · Phase 1 FOUND→CAP Next: EM 610 Culture & Safety

Aap abhi Koch pe ~5 engineers ke Technical Manager ho — strong .NET, microservices, identity. GenAI Phase 1 complete (ya near-complete) hai. EM 609 sawaal yeh hai: jab org “we need GenAI capacity” bole, aap kis shape mein log lagate ho? Platform team alag? Har product squad mein “AI person”? Vendor wrap? Ya ek ML PhD hire karke hope?

Large-org EM interviews (Apple/Walmart-class) is topic pe depth maangte hain: kaun eval owns karta hai, cost/risk kahan baithta hai, aur backend-strong managers ML specialists ke saath kaise pair hote hain. Yeh lesson staffing + sequencing + hiring bar pe focused hai — culture EM 610, incidents EM 611, interview narrative EM 612.

Recall · Phase 1 sequencing · EM 608

Gurukul ne Phase 1 mein order force kiya: prompts/APIs → eval → RAG → agents → prod cost/guardrails → fine-tune later. EM 608 ne technical judgment sikhaya: build/buy, RAG vs FT, when not to ship. EM 609 wohi sequencing org design mein translate karta hai — pehle eval ownership assign karo, phir RAG/FT headcount freeze.

1. Platform team vs application squads

Do common shapes. Platform / enablement: shared model gateway, prompt registry, eval harness, cost meters, guardrail libraries, vector infra contracts. App / product squads: domain RAG, agent workflows, UX, business SLOs. Platform bina app = empty kitchen. App bina platform = har team apna gateway, apna cost surprise, apna “we’ll eval later”.

5-person TM reality se scale karte hue: shuru mein thin platform slice (1–2 engineers part-time + EM ownership of standards) + 1–2 app use-cases. 12-person world (EM Challenge) mein explicit split zaroori ho jata hai — warna “everyone does everything” mein koi eval owner nahi rehta.

2. Eval ownership pehle — RAG / fine-tune baad mein

Phase 1 sequencing leadership pe bhi apply hoti hai. Agar aap pehle RAG headcount aur FT GPU budget approve karte ho, aur eval owner blank chhodte ho, team “shipped a chatbot” celebrate karegi jab metrics protocol missing hoga.

Practical rule for EMs: hire / assign eval + golden-set owner (can be a strong backend engineer with APP 304 muscle) before you staff a dedicated retrieval or fine-tune lane. Platform can own the harness; app owns labels and pass/fail for their domain. Fine-tune (PROD 402) tab discuss karo jab eval beat clear ho aur RAG / prompt path exhaust ho.

Pitfall

“We’ll hire an ML scientist, then decide the product.” Order ulta hai. Pehle problem + eval beat + data residency constraints; phir skills mix. Doosra: har squad ko alag vector DB + alag gateway — cost aur incident blast radius explode.

3. Hiring: GenAI glitter vs fundamentals

GenAI hiring mein do false poles. Pole A: “must have published LLM papers” for every IC who will wire RAG to ServiceNow. Pole B: “anyone who did a ChatGPT demo is AI.” EM bar (EM 604 extended): fundamentals pehle — systems design, APIs, observability, security, testing — phir GenAI layer: eval literacy, prompt/version discipline, tool allowlists, cost awareness.

Aapke background se advantage: identity (SAML/OAuth), microservices, RabbitMQ — yeh GenAI copilots ke hard edges hain. Interview loops mein “tell me about transformers” se zyada weight do: “how would you evaluate retrieval for runbooks?” aur “where does the agent halt?”

Further reading · hiring & org design

First Round Review on building teams and hiring managers — review.firstround.com (search hiring / engineering management essays). Google re:Work hiring guides — rework.withgoogle.com. Use as frameworks; do not paste their text into your interview packet.

4. Vendor vs build — EM owns the decision frame

Build = own gateway, eval, RAG stack, maybe model hosting. Buy = managed model API, managed vector, copilot SaaS. Hybrid = buy model inference, build orchestration + eval + tenancy. EM judgment: lock-in, data residency, latency, unit economics, exit plan. “Vendor is faster” true ho sakta hai — lekin eval aur cost attribution abhi bhi your job hai. SaaS ke andar soft budgets (PROD 404 lesson) tumhare finance surprise nahi rokengi.

5. Cost and risk ownership — named humans

Spreadsheet pe “AI budget” se incident response nahi hota. Name karo: cost owner (meters, budgets, circuit policy), safety / guardrail owner, eval owner, on-call owner for the GenAI surface. Early stage mein ek person multiple hats pehen sakta hai — hats invisible nahi hone chahiye. Exec updates mein “we’re exploring GenAI” ki jagah “eval beat X, monthly token ceiling Y, residual risk Z” bolo.

6. Pairing backend-strong managers with ML specialists

Aap ML researcher nahi banoge overnight — aur EM role mein woh requirement bhi nahi. Healthy pattern: you own roadmap, staffing, eval gates, cost/risk, stakeholder truth; ML specialist (staff/principal IC or shared platform scientist) owns model selection depth, training/PEFT feasibility, eval science edge cases. Weekly joint review: “does the metric move?” not “which paper dropped.”

Conflict signal: specialist wants FT week-1; you want golden set. Resolve with EM 608 judgment + this lesson’s sequencing. Mutual benefit (Koch MBM flavor, detail EM 610): specialist gets serious data/problems; org gets production discipline instead of lab vanity.

Hands-on · staffing sketch (no code)

One page: current 5-person map → proposed GenAI platform slice + one app squad. Columns: role, % GenAI, owns (eval/cost/safety/delivery), hire vs upskill. Bring this into EM Challenge later (5→12 redesign).

Portfolio-facing English · Decision stub

Staff GenAI capacity as platform enablement plus app outcome owners, not a single unlabeled “AI team.” Assign eval ownership before RAG or fine-tune headcount. Hire for systems fundamentals first, then GenAI literacy (eval, halt, cost). Keep build-vs-buy framed by residency, lock-in, and unit economics — and name humans for cost and residual risk. Pair backend-strong managers with ML specialists so delivery discipline and model depth both exist.

Self-check · formative only

1. Platform vs app — ownership farq?

Reveal answer

Platform: shared standards, gateway, harness, meters. App: domain quality, golden sets, user-facing ship risk. Dono named owners chahiye.

2. Eval pehle kyun, RAG/FT baad?

Reveal answer

Bina beat ke RAG/FT headcount demos produce karti hai, production confidence nahi. Phase 1 sequencing org design pe bhi apply hota hai.

3. GenAI hire mein fundamentals ka role?

Reveal answer

Systems, security, testing, APIs pehle; GenAI layer (eval, halt, cost) uske upar. Glitter-only hire platform risk badhata hai.

4. Backend EM + ML specialist pairing ka point?

Reveal answer

EM: delivery, risk, stakeholders, gates. Specialist: model science depth. Joint review on metrics, not paper hype.

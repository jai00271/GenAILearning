EM 603 · Coaching & Feedback · Phase 2 Leadership

Feedback der se dena kindness nahi — coaching OS se growth plans aur hard truths dono chal’te hain

Track: EM Prereq: EM 602 operating system Focus: SBI · coaching vs directing · growth · safety Next: EM 604 · Hiring Bar

Jai, mentoring aap pehle se karte ho. EM 603 mentoring ko enterprise-grade people development mein upgrade karta hai: observable feedback, situational coaching, documented growth, aur psychological safety jo “nice team” se alag — high standards + high support combo.

Large-org directors yeh sun’na chahte hain: aapne kab kisi ko clearly bataya ki level miss ho raha hai, aur kab kisi ko stretch assignment se promote-ready banaya — bina surprise annual review ke.

Recall · EM 602

1:1s feedback ke liye natural slot hain. Agar cadence stable nahi, coaching random pep talks ban jaati hai. Pehle OS, phir conversation craft.

1. Coaching vs directing — situational leadership lite

Har moment pe “Socratic coach” banna slow aur fake lagta hai. Har moment pe “main bataata hoon” banna atrophy banata hai. Situation dekho:

Report state Your mode Example (identity / services context)
New to problem, high stakes (prod auth outage) Direct + teach “Pehle cert rollbacks check karo; main bridge pe rahunga.”
Capable, ambiguous design choice Coach (questions) “OAuth callback failure pe tumhare 2 options — tradeoffs kya hain?”
Senior, owns domain Delegate + consult “Tum decide karo; mujhe risk summary kal tak.”
Motivation dip, skills OK Support / listen “Load / meaning / conflict — kahan ghisai hai?”
Intuition

Coaching = unke dimaag ko strong banana. Directing = abhi sahi outcome ensure karna. EM maturity: consciously choose, default habit se nahi.

2. SBI feedback — Situation · Behavior · Impact

Vague feedback (“be more proactive”) se log confuse hote hain. SBI concrete rakhta hai:

  • Situation: kab / kahan (context)
  • Behavior: observable action (not personality diagnosis)
  • Impact: team / customer / system pe asar

Critique example (Hinglish rehearsal → English delivery):

“Tuesday ke incident bridge pe (S), jab SRE ne queue lag pucha, tumne 4 minute tak guess kiya bina metrics dashboard khole (B). Isse bridge pe duplicate hypotheses chalín aur MTTR roughly 15 min extra gaya (I). Agli baar pehle dashboard link paste, phir hypothesis — chalega?”

Praise example (specific, not fluff):

“Friday ke design review mein (S), tumne SAML assertion skew ke liye clock drift failure mode explicitly likha (B). Security partner ne seedha approve kiya — ek follow-up meeting bach gayi (I). Yeh bar-raising detail thi.”

Pitfall · personality labels

“Tum defensive ho” / “tum quiet ho” — yeh feedback nahi, diagnosis hai. Behavior: “jab challenge aaya, tumne meeting mein response cut kar diya.” Impact: “team ne risk raise karna band kar diya.” Fixable.

3. Praise / critique ratio myths

Internet pe “5:1 positive” magic ratio ghumta hai. Reality: mechanical sandwich (praise–hit–praise) log detect kar lete hain — trust toot’ta hai. Better model:

  • Frequent small feedback both ways — ratio naturally healthy rehta hai.
  • Hard feedback alone bhi valid hai; fake praise se mat wrap karo.
  • Positive feedback bhi SBI — “great job” empty calorie hai.
  • Public praise, private critique — default; exceptions rare (safety issues).

4. Growth plans jo promotion fairy tales nahi

Enterprise EM growth plan = living doc, quarterly refresh:

  1. Current level evidence — projects, scope, complexity.
  2. Target skills / next level behaviors — org ladder language.
  3. 2–3 stretch assignments — owned outcomes, not shadowing forever.
  4. Support you will provide — intros, air cover, course time.
  5. Check-in dates — evidence review, not vibes.
Hands-on · one growth plan this month

Apne 5 mein se ek report ke saath 45-min session: ladder se 3 behaviors pick karo, ek stretch outcome define karo (e.g., “own identity incident retros for Q”), success metrics likho. Aapka role: unblock + feedback, unka role: drive.

Director lens

Promo packet mein EM ka letter tab strong hota hai jab pehle se written growth evidence ho. Last-week scramble (“they’re ready, trust me”) calibration mein weak padta hai — especially Walmart/Apple-class rigor pe.

5. Psychological safety — intro for EMs (not therapy cosplay)

Amy Edmondson sense mein safety: interpersonal risk lene ka comfort — “main galat ho sakta hoon,” “main bad news laata hoon,” “main junior hoker challenge karta hoon.” Safety ≠ low standards. High-performing teams: safe to speak + accountable for outcomes.

  • Blameless postmortems with owned actions (person pe nahi, system pe).
  • Aap pehle vulnerability model karo: “Maine yeh call galat kiya…”
  • Shoot-the-messenger punish mat karo — kabhi, publicly.
  • Safety ke naam pe chronic underperformance ignore mat karo (EM 605 territory).

6. Conversation scripts (rehearse aloud)

A. Early performance concern

“Main tumhare saath early baat karna chahta hoon, surprise review nahi. Last 3 on-call handoffs mein runbook updates miss hue — impact yeh ki next engineer ne same failure dobara debug kiya. Main chahta hoon next 4 weeks har Sev-2 ke baad 24h mein notes update. Main kaise support karun — pairing time? Template?”

B. Stretch invite

“Next quarter identity migration ka tech brief tum lead karo. Success = written RFC + two stakeholder aligns + rollback section. Main air cover dunga product date pressure pe. Interested?”

C. Upward feedback invite (you model safety)

“Meri ek habit jo team slow karti ho — ek example do. Main defensive nahi hounga; main note karke next 1:1 mein follow-up dunga.”

7. Anti-patterns checklist

  • Feedback hoard until calibration week
  • Praise only extroverts / demo heroes; quiet reliability ignore
  • Coach forever without ever directing on safety/compliance
  • Growth plan = course catalog shopping list, no owned outcome
  • “Family” rhetoric se hard truths avoid
Optional paid

Manager Tools or similar feedback/coaching audio libraries — useful drills. Free path bhi kaafi: weekly SBI practice + one recorded mock (self) with a peer EM.

Check yourself

1. SBI ke teen parts kya hain — aur personality label kyun avoid?

Reveal answer

Situation, Behavior, Impact. Personality labels un-observable aur un-fixable feel karte hain; behaviors change ho sakte hain.

2. Kab direct karo, kab coach?

Reveal answer

High stakes / low familiarity → direct; capable + ambiguous → coach; expert owner → delegate; motivation issue → support.

3. Feedback sandwich kyun weak tool hai?

Reveal answer

Log pattern detect karke praise ko fake treat karte hain; trust erodes. Clean SBI + separate genuine praise better.

4. Psychological safety ka matlab low bar nahi — explain.

Reveal answer

Safety = interpersonal risk OK (bad news, challenge, mistakes). High-perf teams iske saath high standards + accountability rakhte hain.

Definition of Done

  • Is hafte 2 SBI feedbacks diye (1 praise, 1 critique) — written notes rakho
  • Ek report ke saath living growth plan co-authored
  • Situational mode consciously choose kiya at least once (noted in 1:1 doc)
  • Team se upward feedback invite kiya; ek change commit kiya
  • Safety vs standards quadrant pe self-assess: team kahan hai?

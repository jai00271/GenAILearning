EM 602 · Operating System · Phase 2 Leadership

EM ka “framework” slides nahi — weekly rhythm, 1:1 OS, aur calendar jo reports ka deep work bachaye

Track: EM Prereq: EM 601 role shift Focus: 1:1s · rituals · triage · maker vs manager Next: EM 603 · Coaching & Feedback

EM 601 ne role delta clear kiya. Ab systems. Jai, aap pehle se team lead karte ho — isliye yahan “meeting kya hoti hai” nahi. Yahan operating system hai: predictable rituals jo 5 engineers ke saath bhi scale karte hain, aur Apple/Walmart-class orgs mein 8–12 reports + hiring load pe toot’te nahi.

Operating system ka test simple hai: agar aap do hafta leave pe ho, kya team ke decisions, escalations, aur growth conversations ruki rahengi? Agar haan — aap process nahi, bottleneck ho.

Recall · EM 601

People bucket ~40–50% intentionally. Hero-coding shrink. Yeh module us time ko structures mein lock karta hai — warna calendar chaos people time ko kha jaata hai.

1. Weekly rhythm (5-engineer squad ke liye baseline)

Rhythm copy-paste mat karna — principles same, slots local. Baseline jo large-org EMs recognize karte hain:

Cadence Ritual Purpose Anti-pattern
Weekly 1:1 with each report (30–45 min) Trust, unblock, career, feedback Status update dump (standup duplicate)
Weekly Staff / team meeting (45–60 min) Shared context, decisions, risks Slide theater; no decisions
2–3× / week Triage / intake (15–25 min) Incoming work, incidents, interrupts Every ping becomes a meeting
Biweekly Planning / refinement ownership check Capacity honesty vs roadmap EM alone estimates everything
Monthly Skip-level (if you have leads) / career deep-dive Signal below your direct layer Surprise inspections; trust break
Quarterly Growth plan refresh + calibration prep Levels, evidence, promo readiness Annual-only surprise reviews
Director lens

Strong EM candidates apna operating cadence 60 seconds mein describe karte hain — “weekly 1:1s never slip, staff for decisions, triage for interrupts, quarterly growth docs.” Weak candidates sirf “open door policy” bolte hain. Open door ≠ operating system.

2. 1:1 agenda template (status meeting nahi)

Manager Tools style principle: 1:1 report ki meeting hai. Aap listen + coach + unblock. Standup already status le chuka hai — duplicate mat karo.

Shared doc (running notes) — suggested sections:

  1. Their agenda first (10–15 min) — jo unhe lean karna hai.
  2. Unblocks / decisions needed from you (5–10).
  3. Feedback both ways (5–10) — chhota, frequent (EM 603 deepen).
  4. Career / growth crumb (5) — har week thoda; quarterly deep.
  5. Admin — PTO, training, hiring help — end mein.
Hands-on · script openers

Start lines (Hinglish ok in private 1:1s; enterprise orgs mein English default): “Aaj tumhari list pe kya hai?” · “Last week se koi cheez jo ab bhi heavy feel ho rahi?” · “Mujhse ek decision chahiye kya?” · “Ek chhoti feedback — maine notice kiya…” Cancel tab hi jab PTO; “busy sprint” se 1:1 cancel = trust debt.

3. Skip-levels — signal without undermining

Agar aapke under ek tech lead / senior EM path pe log hain, skip-levels useful hain. Agar flat 5 ICs hain, aap already direct ho — skip-level ki jagah cross-team listening (peer teams, product partners) zyada valuable.

  • Announce purpose: “Main system health samajhna chahta hoon, tumhare manager ko bypass karke secret police nahi.”
  • Themes pucho, gossip nahi: clarity of goals, psychological safety, load.
  • Patterns ko aggregate karke manager ke saath privately discuss — individuals name-drop mat karo jab tak safety issue na ho.
Pitfall · skip-level as court of appeals

Har disagreement pe skip-level = middle manager kill. Direct report ko pehle coach karo escalate kaise kare; aap exception path ho, default path nahi.

4. Staff meeting jo kaam aaye

Staff / team sync ka job: shared reality + decisions. Agenda board pe rakho:

  • Decisions needed (DRI named)
  • Risks / incidents learning (blameless, action-owned)
  • Cross-person dependencies (identity service ↔ orders queue, etc.)
  • Hiring / capacity headlines (2 minutes)
  • Wins (30–60 sec — morale without fluff)

GenAI / platform work ke liye explicitly: “eval gate status” ek standing line ban sakti hai — CAP/PROD track se aapka edge: demos ko ritual mein measure karo.

5. Triage: interrupts ka valve

Microservices + RabbitMQ + identity world mein interrupts aate rehte hain — prod auth failures, poison messages, partner SAML changes. Bina triage ke har Slack ping context switch ban jaata hai.

  1. Single intake (channel or board column): Severity + owner + next update time.
  2. EM / rotating lead 15-min triage: accept, defer, or schedule spike.
  3. Anything >2 days → visible backlog item with capacity tradeoff called out.

6. Maker vs manager schedule — reports protect karo pehle

Aapka calendar fragmented hoga — accept. Reports ka nahi hona chahiye by default. Practical moves:

  • Cluster 1:1s same afternoons; don’t spray across mornings.
  • Stakeholder meetings pe aap attend; poori team ko CC-meeting mein mat ghaseeto.
  • “Focus hours” team agreement — Slack norms: async first, @here rare.
  • Hiring loops pe interviewers ko prep blocks do; back-to-back 5 interviews = quality crash.
Pitfall · calendar as status symbol

Full calendar ≠ important EM. Full calendar with zero think-blocks = reactive coordinator. Friday AM empty for “strategy / writing / hiring debrief” intentionally block karo — pehle se, nahi toh it fills with noise.

7. Written OS > tribal memory

Large orgs documentation expect karte hain. Minimum wiki / Notion page:

  • How we run 1:1s and staff
  • How interrupts are triaged
  • Definition of ready / done (include eval for GenAI work)
  • On-call expectations
  • How to escalate (security, legal, exec)

Yeh “bureaucracy” nahi — onboarding accelerator aur interview artifact (“I built a lightweight team OS”) ban’ta hai.

8. Personal weekly review (30 minutes, non-negotiable)

  1. Kaunse 1:1s slipped? Why?
  2. Kis report ko feedback due hai?
  3. Hiring pipeline health?
  4. Roadmap vs capacity lie detect hua kya?
  5. Ek relationship (peer/stakeholder) jo cold pad raha hai?
Hands-on · OS install this week

(1) Shared 1:1 doc template 5 reports ke liye. (2) Staff agenda pinned. (3) Triage slot calendar pe. (4) Ek maker-morning block team-wide announce. Agle retrospect mein poocho: “OS se kya better / worse hua?”

Check yourself

1. 1:1 ko standup se kaise alag rakhein?

Reveal answer

Report-owned agenda, career/feedback/unblocks — status theater nahi; standup already covers board status.

2. Triage ka EM-level purpose kya hai?

Reveal answer

Interrupts ko visible capacity tradeoffs banana — silent yes se roadmap chori rokna.

3. Maker schedule protect karne ka concrete move?

Reveal answer

1:1s/stakeholder meetings batch karke afternoons; mornings deep work; poori team ko har sync mein na ghaseetna.

4. “Open door” kyun OS substitute nahi?

Reveal answer

Open door reactive + uneven access deta hai; OS proactive cadence + fairness + continuity jab manager absent ho.

Definition of Done

  • Weekly rhythm table apni team ke liye customize karke calendar pe blocks hain
  • Har report ke liye shared 1:1 doc + template sections live
  • Staff agenda + triage slot running for ≥2 weeks
  • Team wiki: “How we work” page with escalation + interrupt rules
  • Personal Friday review checklist used at least twice

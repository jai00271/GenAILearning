EM 611 · Incident Leadership & Operational Excellence

Incident pe EM = IC + communicator — panic theater nahi

Track: EM · Phase 2 Audience: Technical Manager → Engineering Manager Prereq: EM 610 Culture & Safety Next: EM 612 EM Interview Narrative

Microservices + identity scale (Dell-era themes) aur ServiceNow automation se aap already “ops serious” ho. GenAI add karta hai naye failure modes: prompt regression, retrieval poisoning vibes, cost runaway, hallucinated auth guidance. EM 611: severity, IC role for managers, exec updates, postmortems, on-call health, SLO conversations — Google SRE / public incident guides ke links ke saath, copyrighted text copy kiye bina.

Recall · EM 610 · PROD 404

Safety climate (610) bina postmortem theater ban jati hai. Observability/cost (PROD 404) metrics deti hai — EM un metrics ko people + process se connect karta hai: kaun IC hai, kab page, kab customer notify.

1. Manager as Incident Commander (when needed)

IC role: coordinate, prioritize, clear airtime, protect deep workers, track actions, decide escalate. Kabhi IC engineer hota hai; kabhi EM — especially jab stakeholder noise IC ke focus ko tod rahi ho. Rule of thumb: agar aap typing fix kar rahe ho aur koi coordinate nahi kar raha, role flip karo.

2. Severity — impact first, ego later

Draft a simple Sev matrix for GenAI surfaces: user harm (bad advice in auth/HR), data exposure risk, widespread vs single-tenant, cost burn rate, regulatory path. Identity-adjacent GenAI wrongness often warrants higher Sev than “chat tone weird.” Document once; train the team; don’t invent Sev in the heat of the night.

Pitfall

Sev inflate for exec theater, ya Sev deflate to “avoid looking bad.” Mutual Benefit angle: customers + employees deserve accurate severity. Hidden Sev-1s destroy trust faster than an honest bridge call.

3. Exec communication cadence

Template (adapt): What we know / don’t know / next check-in / customer impact / mitigation in flight / ask (decision or air cover). Short. Timestamped. No blame. Jai-style credibility: ServiceNow automation + delivery discipline se aaya trust — incident updates usi crispness se likho, metrics fabricate mat karo.

Portfolio-facing English · Exec update stub

Draft a 8–10 line Sev-2 update for a GenAI assistant that started citing stale identity policy. Include knowns, unknowns, mitigation, next update time, and one explicit ask. Keep it usable for a VP skim.

4. Postmortems that change systems

Timeline, contributing factors, what went well, action items with owners + dates. Link to blameless culture (EM 610) but track action completion. GenAI-specific actions often: freeze prompt, restore previous prompt hash, expand golden set, add cost anomaly alert, clarify eval owner (EM 609).

5. On-call health & SLO conversations

Unsustainable pages = attrition + silent risk. EM owns staffing for coverage and toil reduction (automation — aapke ServiceNow themes yahan authentic story hain: reduce human toil, don’t invent fake MTTR numbers). SLO talks with product: which GenAI quality/latency targets are customer-promised vs internal stretch.

Hands-on · Mini severity card

Ek page Sev card likho: Sev-1..3 definitions for your GenAI + identity-adjacent surfaces, IC/Comms defaults, and exec update cadence. Review with one peer.

Director lens

Directors score: calm updates, clear ownership, learning that ships. Panic Slack + no action owners = junior signal. Jai path: reuse delivery/ops credibility; GenAI failures pe same operational excellence.

Self-check · formative only

1. IC vs fixer conflict kab hota hai?

Reveal answer

Jab coordination missing ho aur managers/ICs deep debugging mein lose ho jayein — explicit IC protect karta hai fixers ka airtime.

2. GenAI severity mein kya alag socho?

Reveal answer

Harmful advice (esp. identity/security), data risk, blast radius, cost burn — not only HTTP 500 counts.

3. Exec update mein kya hona chahiye?

Reveal answer

Knowns, unknowns, impact, mitigation, next check-in, explicit ask — short, timestamped, no blame theater.

4. Postmortem success signal?

Reveal answer

Owned actions with dates that land (alerts, eval gates, prompt freeze) — not only a well-written doc.

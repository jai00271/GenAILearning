Title: Kafka consumer lag on payments.events
Symptoms: lag climbing above 50k messages for > 10 minutes

Checks:
1. Consumer group payments-ledger-v3 — are all members alive?
2. Disk saturation on broker rack B (historical cause of stall)
3. Poison message: look for deserialization errors in payments-ledger logs
4. Scale consumers horizontally only after confirming partition count allows it

If lag is isolated to one partition, prefer key redistribution planning over blind restart.
Notify #payments-risk if checkout latency SLO burns error budget.

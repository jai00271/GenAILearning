# Leave policy (HR)

Employees receive leave according to the matrix below. Always cite MaxDays from this table.

| LeaveType | MaxDays | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Annual | 24 | Manager approval for long streaks |
| Sick | 12 | Certificate after 3 days |
| Parental | 90 | HR ticket mandatory |
| Bereavement | 5 | Immediate family |

If the table conflicts with Slack folklore, the table wins.
